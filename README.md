# Caughley Universe

An endless lattice of lights, dark until your voice sends a wave through it.

One page serves phone, PC and headset:

- https://mcaughley.github.io/CaughleyUniverse/
- `vr.html` is an identical copy, kept so older links work.

## Soundscapes

- crystal, crystal-loop: the sound of teamLab's *The Infinite Crystal Universe* (teamLab, 2018; sound: teamLab), looped. Used with teamLab's permission, as advised by the site owner.

The recordings in `sounds/` were supplied by the project owner as free, royalty-free downloads.

## Sky photographs

The images in `sky/` were supplied by the project owner and have been resized and edge-faded for use in the scene.
They appear to be space-agency composite images; source and credit lines are to be confirmed by the owner.

Credits known so far, from NASA's own pages:

- pillars, cliffs, helix-knots: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI
- pillars-cloud: a softened, star-free copy of pillars, used to colour the nebula clouds
- whirlpool: NASA, ESA, S. Beckwith (STScI), and the Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA)
- helix: NASA, NOAO, ESA, the Hubble Helix Nebula Team, M. Meixner (STScI), and T.A. Rector (NRAO)

The remaining images are still to be confirmed.
