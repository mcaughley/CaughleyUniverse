PULSE LATTICE

A lattice of lights that never ends, dark until your voice wakes it. Turn and tilt your phone to look around. You drift the way you face: touch and hold to travel faster.

Headphones make it feel much bigger, and let you switch on the echo of your own voice. Allow the microphone when asked: your voice is only used live on this phone. Nothing is recorded or sent.

40+40

This pulses the lights and the tone 40 times a second.

Do not use it if you, or anyone who can see the screen, has epilepsy or is sensitive to flashing light. Stop if you feel unwell.

It is an art effect. It is not a medical device or a treatment.
