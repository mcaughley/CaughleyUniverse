PULSE LATTICE VR

A lattice of lights that never ends, dark until your voice wakes it. Choose your headset, then Start. Set things up on the next screen, then press Enter VR.

Allow the microphone when asked: your voice is only used live on this device. Nothing is recorded or sent.
